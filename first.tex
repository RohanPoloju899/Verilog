	Proof that $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational.
	
	Suppose that $\sqrt{2}$ is rational. Then
	
	\[
	\sqrt{2} = \frac{p}{q}
	\]
	
	where $p$ and $q$ are integers with no common factor.
	
	Squaring both sides gives
	
	\[
	2 = \frac{p^2}{q^2}
	\]
	
	and therefore
	
	\[
	p^2 = 2q^2.
	\]
	
	Hence, $p^2$ is even, so $p$ is even.
	
	Let $p = 2k$. Then
	
	\[
	4k^2 = 2q^2
	\]
	
	so
	
	\[
	q^2 = 2k^2.
	\]
	
	Thus, $q$ is also even.
	
	Therefore, both $p$ and $q$ are even, which is a contradiction because they have no common factor.
	
	Hence,
	
	\[
	\sqrt{2}\text{ is irrational.}
	\]
